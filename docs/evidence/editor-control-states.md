# Editor control states

Chromium at 1720 × 1050 on the synthetic Northlight interview. The audit enumerated controls, focus targets, and pointer handlers in the editor chrome. Each row records a pointer pass, a held press, and keyboard focus where available. Ctrl+4 focuses the timeline for clip and transition keyboard editing. Clip bodies, transitions, and gain handles are DOM hit targets over the filmstrip and waveform canvas.

The before build is `e7001a00`. The after build is based on `a12eccd4`. A sticky track header obscured some hit targets as the playhead moved. Those rows are marked in the tables. The after pass also hovered the parent clip before trying its fade handles.
